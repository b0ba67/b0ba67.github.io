# Infection Characteristics
- It is a retrovirus
	- meaning it makes contact with a cell's membrane and... does shit 
	- from wht i can understand.. so the castle explanation is
		- it can copy the dna of its host rly well and essentially like replace the parts that it wants to with its own like virus but in a way that fits the host dna well.
		- by doing this it can hijack the cells original function and as the cell reproduces it makes more of this now fucked up version of the cell without the virus itself having to do any more work
		- at the same time this newly fucked up cell can go and fuck up other cells it makes contact w - same as the virus itself can and its a repeat of this cycle
			- explains why people turn insanely fast
- the most common "disease" caused by the T-virus is "Cannibal disease"
	- the hosts mutate to gain enhanced survival capabilities
	- but also gets severe brain damage and violent urges
	- at an advanced stage they are basically zombies
		- ppl w early symptoms can actually b treated w antiviral medication and cured
- in extremely rare cases (1/1mil) people can be infected but get minimal/no brain damage
